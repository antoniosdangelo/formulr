FormulrGet the add-in

AI answers inside your Excel formulas

Type a question in a cell, then =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2) next to it. Formulr runs on your own AI key, so your answers are yours and so is the bill.

Download the add-in

Free to install. Works in Excel on the web and desktop. No sign up, no access code.

You use your own AI key

  • Formulr never uses anyone else's AI key. You paste your own, and your own AI account pays for your answers.
  • Your key stays on your device. It is sent over a secure connection with each answer and is never saved on the server.
  • Without your own key, Formulr gives no answers. There is no shared key to fall back on.
  • Press Remove key in the Formulr pane to delete it from your device at any time.

Set up in three steps

  1. Get the add-in

    Download formulr-manifest.xml. It is a small file that tells Excel where Formulr lives.

  2. Upload it in Excel

    In Excel open any workbook, then go to Insert, Add-ins, More Add-ins, My Add-ins, Manage My Add-ins, Upload My Add-in, and choose the file.

  3. Add your AI key

    Click Formulr on the Home tab. Under Your AI key, pick your provider, paste your key and press Save key. Then type =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2) next to a question.

Questions

Do I need an account with Formulr?

No. There is no sign up and no access code. You need Excel and an AI key from Anthropic or Google Gemini.

Who pays for the answers?

You do, through your own AI account. Each question uses one call on your key. Set a monthly spending limit with your provider before you start.

Does it work in desktop Excel?

Yes, in Excel on the web and in desktop Excel with any Microsoft account. An uploaded add-in is remembered per browser, so repeat step 2 on a new browser or laptop.

What if I see #NAME?

Wait a minute, then reload the page or reopen the workbook. If it stays, remove older Formulr entries in Manage My Add-ins and upload the file again.

Can it read pictures?

Yes. Click a floating picture and press Answer picture in the Formulr pane, or paste a screenshot into the paste box there.

How do I get help?

In the Formulr pane press Run checks, then Copy, and send the report to whoever helps you. It never includes your AI key.

Privacy

  • Your questions go to the AI provider you picked, and only to answer them. Formulr keeps no copy of your questions or answers on the server.
  • Your AI key is kept in the add-in's storage in your browser. The server uses it for one call and never logs or stores it.
  • Finished answers are kept in your browser for a week so reopening a workbook does not ask again.
  • Do not send data you are not allowed to share, such as confidential company files, until you have approval.
