Your AI key

Formulr never uses anyone else's key. No key, no answers.

Formulr runs on your own AI account. Pick your provider and paste your key once. It stays on this device only and is sent over a secure connection with each answer. It is never saved on the server.

Only a signed, expiring pass is kept on this device, never the code itself. Untick it on a shared computer, or press Lock to forget it.

Formula

Type =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2) in the cell next to a question. You get just the answer in that one cell. For multiple choice it always picks one option, the closest if none match exactly.

Add text after it for an instruction, like =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2, "in Spanish"). A range such as B2:F9 works for a pasted block.

Picture

Place the picture over the cells, click it, then press Answer picture on the Home ribbon (or below). The answer appears just right of the picture. It answers only the picture you click, one at a time. A picture inside a cell cannot be read by Excel's add-in tools: copy it (Ctrl+C) and paste it in the box below instead.

Select the cell for the answer, copy the picture (a screenshot, or a picture in a cell), click here and press Ctrl+V. The answer goes in the cell just right of the selected cell.

Add Formulr to another laptop

Download the install file, then in Excel go to Insert, Add-ins, Manage My Add-ins, Upload My Add-in. Or send the install link, which has every step.
