Install Formulr in one minute

Works in Excel on the web and in desktop Excel, with any Microsoft account. You need the access code from whoever runs this service, and your own AI key from Anthropic or Google Gemini.

You use your own AI key

  • Formulr never uses anyone else's AI key. You paste your own, and your own AI account pays for your answers.
  • Your key stays on your device. It is sent over a secure connection with each answer and is never saved on the server.
  • Without your own key, Formulr gives no answers. There is no shared key.
  • No GitHub or Cloudflare account is needed. Just Excel and your key.
  1. Download the file.

    Download formulr-manifest.xml

  2. Open Excel (Excel on the web or the desktop app) and open any workbook.
  3. Add it. Go to Insert, then Add-ins, then More Add-ins, then My Add-ins, then Manage My Add-ins, then Upload My Add-in. Choose the file you just downloaded.
  4. Unlock it. On the Home tab click Formulr, enter the access code once, and leave "Remember on this device" ticked.
  5. Add your AI key. In the same pane, under Your AI key, pick your provider, paste your key and press Save key. It stays on your device only, and your own AI account pays for your answers.
  6. Try it. Type a question in B2, then in C2 type =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2). The answer fills C2 and a short explanation fills D2. Keep C2 and D2 empty first.

If something looks wrong

  • Remove any older Formulr, AI Helper or Excel AI Helper entries in Manage My Add-ins, reload the page, and upload the file again.
  • If you see #NAME?, wait a minute, reload the page, or reopen the workbook.
  • In the Formulr pane press Run checks, then Copy, and send the report to whoever runs this service. It never includes your access code or your AI key.
  • Uploaded add-ins are remembered per browser. On another laptop or browser, repeat these steps.

Content you send is processed by the AI provider you picked. Do not use confidential company data until you have approval to.
