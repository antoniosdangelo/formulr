Bond basics
- Price = the sum of each payment divided by (1 + y/m)^k, where y is the yield, m the payments per year and k the payment number.
- A coupon below the yield means the bond sells at a discount; above means a premium; equal means par.
- Use the payment frequency the question states. If it states none, assume annual payments.

Worked example (a check on your own method)
- 3-year bond, face 1,000, 5% annual coupon, 6% yield: price = 50/1.06 + 50/1.06^2 + 1,050/1.06^3 = 973.27.
