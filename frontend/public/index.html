Formulr

One Excel formula, =FORMULR.ANSWER, plus picture buttons.

Install in one minute

If your organization already added it

Open Excel and look for Formulr on the Home tab. Click it, paste your own AI key under Your AI key, and enter the access code once. Tick "Remember on this device" so you do not need to enter it again.

To try it yourself in this browser

Download the manifest, then in Excel on the web choose Insert, Add-ins, More Add-ins, My Add-ins, Manage My Add-ins, Upload My Add-in. This only lasts for this browser. Ask your admin to deploy it to everyone so nobody has to upload anything.

Needs an access code from the person who runs this service, and your own AI key from Anthropic or Google Gemini. Formulr never uses anyone else's key, and yours stays on your device.
