Ask a question in a cell. Get the answer in the next one.

Paste a question into Excel, type =FORMULR.ANSWER(B2) next to it, and the answer appears. Formulr runs on your own AI key.

Download the add-inSee how it works

Formulr never uses anyone else's AI key. Your key stays on your device and is never saved on our server.

Set up in three steps

  1. Get the add-in

    Download formulr-manifest.xml, a small file that tells Excel where Formulr lives.

  2. Upload it in Excel

    In Excel, go to Insert, Add-ins, More Add-ins, My Add-ins, Manage My Add-ins, Upload My Add-in, and pick the file.

  3. Add your AI key

    Get and check your key below, then paste it in the Formulr pane under Your AI key.

Questions

Do I need an account with Formulr?

No. There is no sign up and no access code. You need Excel and an AI key from Anthropic or Google Gemini.

Who pays for the answers?

You do, through your own AI account. Each question is one call on your key. Set a monthly spending limit with your provider first.

Does it work in desktop Excel?

Yes, in Excel on the web and in desktop Excel. An uploaded add-in is remembered per browser, so repeat step 2 on a new browser or laptop.

What if I see #NAME?

Wait a minute, then reload the workbook. If it stays, remove older Formulr entries in Manage My Add-ins and upload the file again.

How do I get help?

In the Formulr pane press Run checks, then Copy, and send the report to whoever helps you. It never includes your AI key.

Privacy

  • Your key stays on your device. Each answer sends it over a secure connection for that one call. It is never logged or stored.
  • Your questions go only to the AI provider you picked. Formulr keeps no copy of questions or answers.
  • Do not send data you are not allowed to share, such as confidential company files.
